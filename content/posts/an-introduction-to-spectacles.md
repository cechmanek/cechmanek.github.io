Welcome to a friendly introduction to the fascinating field of computer vision, in Go.

Spectacles is a computer vision library written for Go, by Go developers. It aims to make computer vision accessible, composable, and safe — the way idiomatic Go code should be.

I've included everything you need to get started. Let's begin!

## Why Go for computer vision?

Go gives you fast compilation, easy concurrency, and a standard library that stays out of your way. For systems that need to process frames in parallel — robotics, surveillance, real-time pipelines — Go's goroutines map naturally onto vision workloads.
