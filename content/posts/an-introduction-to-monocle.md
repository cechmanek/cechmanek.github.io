A friendly introduction to the fascinating field of computer vision, in Rust.

Monocle is a computer vision library written for Rust, by Rust developers. It leans into everything Rust does best: memory safety without a garbage collector, fearless concurrency, and performance you can rely on.

I've included everything you need to get started. Let's begin!

## Why Rust for computer vision?

Rust gives you zero-cost abstractions and guaranteed safety. When you're processing millions of pixels per second, you want both the speed and the confidence that your pipeline won't corrupt memory. Monocle aims to give vision engineers exactly that.
